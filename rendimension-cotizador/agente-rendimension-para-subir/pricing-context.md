# Pricing Context — Rendimension

## Purpose
This file defines how pricing must be interpreted, framed, and validated across all Rendimension outputs.

Rendimension does not price images.
Rendimension prices business outcomes, communication systems, risk reduction, and strategic value.

Visual deliverables such as renders, walkthroughs, VR experiences, diagrams, or presentations are execution tools inside a larger solution.

---

## Core Pricing Principle

Pricing must never be positioned as:
- price per render
- price per image
- low-cost visualization production
- output volume pricing only

Pricing must be positioned as:
- value of solving the client's problem
- level of strategic support required
- complexity of the decision being supported
- business impact of the deliverables
- scope of communication system needed

---

## Strategic Positioning Rule

If a client asks:
- "How much for 3 renders?"
- "What do you charge per image?"
- "Send me a render quote"

The response must not stay at the deliverable level.

The request must be reframed toward:
- What decision needs to be supported?
- What sales or approval objective is being pursued?
- What risk is being reduced?
- What stakeholder communication gap is being solved?

---

## Pricing Logic Hierarchy

Always think in this order:

1. Client objective
2. Business impact
3. Decision risk
4. Scope of strategic support
5. Required visual tools
6. Production effort

Never reverse this order.

---

## What Rendimension Is Actually Pricing

Rendimension prices combinations of the following:

### 1. Sales Acceleration Support
Use when the client needs to:
- pre-sell units
- improve buyer confidence
- market before construction
- create stronger launch materials

### 2. Investor / Stakeholder Confidence Support
Use when the client needs to:
- justify a project
- present to investors
- obtain approvals
- align internal decision-makers

### 3. Pre-Construction Validation Support
Use when the client needs to:
- detect mistakes before building
- validate design intent
- improve coordination
- test alternatives before committing

### 4. Market Positioning Support
Use when the client needs to:
- differentiate the project
- elevate perceived value
- communicate luxury / exclusivity / quality
- support branding and launch narrative

---

## Internal Pricing Framing Rules

When preparing proposals, quotes, scopes, or sales responses:

Do:
- frame pricing around the objective
- explain why the scope exists
- connect visuals to the business result
- describe the work as a strategic package or solution
- justify deliverables as tools, not as the product

Do not:
- lead with number of renders
- reduce the work to "3 images + 1 revision"
- sound like a commodity vendor
- compare the offer to cheap offshore rendering studios
- justify value based only on realism or software quality

---

## Output Language Rules

Preferred terms:
- strategic visualization system
- decision-making asset
- pre-sales support tool
- investor communication package
- approval support material
- pre-construction validation tool
- premium presentation system
- business-facing visualization strategy

Avoid as primary language:
- render service
- image production
- cheap rendering
- price per image
- rendering vendor
- outsourced render provider

---

## Pricing Buckets

The exact number can vary by project, but all pricing should conceptually fall into one of these buckets:

### Bucket A — Targeted Visual Support
For smaller scopes where the client needs focused support for one key communication objective.

Typical use:
- one presentation moment
- one approval need
- one marketing push
- one specific decision point

This is not "small render work."
This is limited-scope strategic support.

### Bucket B — Strategic Sales / Approval Package
For projects needing a stronger communication system with multiple assets working together.

Typical use:
- pre-sales package
- investor deck support
- project launch material
- multiple high-impact views with narrative cohesion

### Bucket C — Full Decision Support System
For larger projects where visuals support multiple business functions at once:
- sales
- approvals
- investor confidence
- internal alignment
- design validation

This must be presented as a high-value strategic engagement.

---

## Proposal Framing Rule

Every proposal should answer:

1. What is the client trying to achieve?
2. Why does this matter commercially?
3. Why is this scope the right scope?
4. How do the deliverables support the objective?
5. Why is Rendimension the right strategic partner?

If any proposal skips these steps and jumps directly to deliverables and price, it is incomplete.

---

## Approval Rule for the Orchestrator

Reject any output that:
- treats pricing as per-image production
- sounds transactional
- centers the conversation on volume
- lacks outcome framing
- could be confused with a low-cost render vendor quote

Approve only outputs that:
- elevate the conversation
- connect pricing to strategic value
- frame deliverables as instruments of business impact
- protect premium positioning
