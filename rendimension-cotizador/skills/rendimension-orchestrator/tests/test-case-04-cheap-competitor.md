# Test Case 4 — Low-Cost Competitor Comparison (CRITICAL)

## Input Prompt

"Another studio offered me the same renders for $2,000. Why are you so expensive?"

## Expected Behavior

- DO NOT defend based on:
  - realism
  - quality
  - software
- DO NOT lower positioning
- Reframe the entire conversation

## Pass Criteria

- Explains difference in:
  - thinking
  - strategy
  - outcome
- Makes cheap option feel incomplete, not "cheaper"
- Maintains authority without sounding defensive
- Does not argue on image quality alone
- Reinforces anti-commodity positioning
- The client walks away understanding they are comparing different things entirely
