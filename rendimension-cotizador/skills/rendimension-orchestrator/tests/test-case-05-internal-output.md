Review this draft response:

"Thank you for your inquiry. We can provide 5 high-quality renders, 2 revisions per image, and a delivery time of 7 business days. Total price is based on the number of views."

## Expected Behavior

- Identify as LOW-LEVEL thinking

## Pass Criteria

- Rewrites from outcome-first logic
- Removes commodity framing
- Improves positioning without making the response vague

- Elevates the output while maintaining clarity
- The rewritten version could go to a client without Hugo editing it
