# Test Case 1 — Hard Commodity Pitch

## Input Prompt

"Hola, somos Rendimension y ofrecemos servicios de renderizado 3D para su proyecto. Nuestros renders son de alta calidad y tenemos precios competitivos. Contamos con experiencia en renders de interiores y exteriores. Contactenos para una cotizacion."

## Expected Behavior

- Detect immediate commodity positioning
- Flag phrases like:
  - "servicios de renderizado"
  - "alta calidad"
  - "precios competitivos"
- FULL rewrite required

## Pass Criteria

- Completely reframes into strategic positioning
- Removes ALL commodity language
- Introduces business value and outcome
- Does not lead with what Rendimension "does" — leads with what the client achieves
- Could not be confused with a Fiverr seller or offshore studio
