## Corrected Output

---

Thank you for reaching out.

Before we talk about scope or timeline, I need to understand what you're actually trying to accomplish.

Are you:

- **Pre-selling units or leasing space?** You need visuals that give buyers confidence before they commit. The goal is faster conversions and fewer financing delays.

- **Presenting to investors or lenders?** You need materials that justify the project — ones that reduce perceived risk and accelerate approval.

- **Aligning internal stakeholders** (architects, contractors, designers, leadership)? You need clear visual language everyone understands the same way.

- **Validating design decisions before construction?** You need to catch misalignments early and test options without committing to builds.

- **Differentiating your project in the market?** You need positioning that elevates perceived value and justifies premium pricing.

Once I understand your real objective, I can structure a focused visualization strategy — not just a list of renders.

The scope, timeline, and investment all follow from the problem you're solving.

Let's start there.

---

## Key Changes

- **Shifted from transaction to strategy:** Removed "here are 5 renders" and replaced with "what problem are we solving?" — puts client needs first and positions Rendimension as a strategic advisor, not a vendor.

- **Embedded outcome framing:** Every option presented maps to a specific business result (sales acceleration, investor confidence, stakeholder alignment, risk reduction, market positioning). This helps the client self-identify their need and justifies premium pricing.

- **Removed all commodity language:** Eliminated "high-quality renders," "price per item" pricing, "revisions per image" delivery mechanics — these are vendor details. Replaced with outcome-focused conversation that protects premium positioning.
