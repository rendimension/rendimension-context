## Key Changes

1. **Outcome-First Framing** — Begins with the investor's actual need (conviction, clarity, confidence) rather than listing what Rendimension produces. Positions the work as risk reduction and decision support, not vendor services.

2. **Reframed Language** — "2 rounds of revisions" becomes "The Clarity Guarantee" (premium positioning); deliverables are introduced as tools serving the investor communication objective, not as a production menu.

3. **Strategic Context** — Explicitly connects the package to investor psychology (moving from evaluation to commitment) and explains why each component exists. Removes commodity language ("photorealistic renders") and replaces with strategic purpose ("Showcase Renderings" positioned to demonstrate competitive advantage, market positioning, etc.).

4. **Premium Tone & Authority** — Closer line ("It's not seeing it. It's knowing it's right.") anchors the slogan. Entire framing positions Rendimension as a strategic partner managing investor communication, not a rendering studio executing a production order.

5. **Price as Strategic Investment** — $18,500 is no longer presented as production cost; it's positioned as an investment in moving a capital raise from "maybe" to "committed."
