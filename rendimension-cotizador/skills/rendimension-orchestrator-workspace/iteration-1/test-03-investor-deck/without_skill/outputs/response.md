# Rendimension Visualization Package - Slide Content Review

## Original Content
"Rendimension Visualization Package: 8 photorealistic renders, 1 virtual tour, 1 floor plan. Total: $18,500. Timeline: 3 weeks. Includes 2 rounds of revisions."

## Review & Feedback

### Strengths
- Clear pricing and timeline information
- Specific deliverable quantities
- Mentions revision rounds (good for client confidence)
- Straightforward structure

### Areas for Improvement
1. **Missing value proposition** - Doesn't communicate WHY these deliverables matter to the client
2. **Weak headline** - Reads as a feature list rather than a compelling offer
3. **Lacks emphasis** - No visual hierarchy to guide attention to key selling points
4. **Generic language** - "Photorealistic renders" could be more specific or impactful
5. **Missing context** - No mention of use cases or applications (investment pitches, marketing, design decisions, etc.)
6. **No differentiators** - Doesn't highlight what makes Rendimension's package special
