RENDIMENSION — RECOMMENDATION ENGINE DATA
Smart Package Suggestions for the Express Estimator
Based on Industry Research — March 2026
============================================================

PURPOSE:
This file contains the research data needed to build the smart
recommendation engine for the homeowner path. The engine maps:
  Project Type + Project Budget → Recommended Visualization Package

The goal: make the suggestion feel proportional and logical.
A $25K kitchen remodel gets suggested $1,000–$2,000 in viz.
A $1M custom home gets suggested $15,000–$40,000.

============================================================
SECTION 1: VISUALIZATION SPEND AS % OF PROJECT BUDGET
============================================================

There is no single published industry standard for "what percentage
of a construction budget goes to visualization." This ratio does not
exist as a tracked metric the way architect fees (8-15% of construction
cost) or marketing budgets (1-1.5% of development value) are tracked.

However, by cross-referencing:
- Architect fees (8-20% of construction cost)
- Marketing budgets for developments (1-1.5% of projected sales)
- Visual content allocation (10-20% of marketing budget)
- Actual studio pricing vs. typical project budgets

...we can derive practical ratios.

DERIVED VISUALIZATION BUDGET RATIOS:

| Project Type              | Budget Range        | Viz Spend %  | Viz $ Range          |
|---------------------------|---------------------|--------------|----------------------|
| Kitchen/bath remodel      | $15K–$50K           | 3–6%         | $500–$2,500          |
| Full home remodel         | $50K–$150K          | 2–4%         | $1,500–$5,000        |
| New custom home           | $150K–$500K         | 1.5–3%       | $3,000–$12,000       |
| Luxury home               | $500K–$2M           | 1–2.5%       | $8,000–$40,000       |
| Commercial/multi-family   | $2M+                | 0.5–1.5%     | $15,000–$100,000+    |
| Interior design project   | $10K–$100K          | 4–8%         | $800–$5,000          |

KEY INSIGHT: The percentage DECREASES as budget increases, but the
absolute dollar amount INCREASES. This is the same pattern as
architect fees — lower % on bigger projects, higher total $.

WHY THESE RATIOS WORK:
- Small projects: Visualization IS the main decision tool. Clients
  spending $25K on a kitchen need to see it first. $1,000–$1,500
  feels reasonable — it is 4-6% of budget, similar to what they
  would spend on design consultation.
- Large projects: Visualization is one tool among many (surveys,
  engineering, legal, marketing). $30K on a $1.5M project is 2% —
  proportional to what developers spend on marketing visuals.

SECTION 2: STANDARD DELIVERABLES BY PROJECT TYPE
============================================================

KITCHEN OR BATHROOM REMODEL ($15K–$50K)
---------------------------------------
Standard scope:
  - 2–3 interior renderings (key angles of the space)
  - 1 material comparison view (optional but high value)
  - NO floor plans needed (existing space)
  - NO animation needed
  - NO VR needed

Why: Small space, few decisions. Client needs to see finishes,
lighting, and layout from 2-3 angles. That is enough to decide.

Industry pricing for this scope:
  - Budget studios: $400–$900 (2-3 basic renders)
  - Mid-market studios: $1,000–$2,000 (2-3 detailed renders)
  - Premium studios: $2,000–$3,500 (2-3 high-end renders)

RENDIMENSION RECOMMENDATION:
  - 2 Standard Renderings ($499 x 2) = $998
  - OR 2 Detailed Renderings ($750 x 2) = $1,500
  - Add 1 Additional Angle if comparing layouts: + $299
  - Range: $998 – $1,799


FULL HOME REMODEL ($50K–$150K)
------------------------------
Standard scope:
  - 3–5 interior renderings (living room, kitchen, master bedroom, bathroom)
  - 1–2 exterior renderings (if facade changes)
  - 1 3D floor plan (optional but useful for layout decisions)
  - NO animation needed for most remodels
  - Browser tour only if major layout changes

Industry pricing for this scope:
  - Budget studios: $1,500–$3,000 (4-6 basic renders)
  - Mid-market studios: $3,000–$6,000 (4-6 detailed renders + floor plan)
  - Premium studios: $5,000–$10,000 (full package)

RENDIMENSION RECOMMENDATION:
  - 3 Standard Renderings ($499 x 3) = $1,497
  - 1 3D Floor Plan ($850) = $850
  - OR 3 Detailed Renderings ($750 x 3) = $2,250 + floor plan
  - Range: $1,497 – $3,100


NEW CUSTOM HOME ($150K–$500K)
-----------------------------
Standard scope:
  - 4–6 interior renderings (all major rooms)
  - 2–3 exterior renderings (front, rear, aerial/context)
  - 1–2 3D floor plans (per level)
  - Video walkthrough or browser tour (1,500–3,000 sqft typical)
  - VR optional but increasingly expected for custom homes

Industry pricing for this scope:
  - Budget studios: $4,000–$8,000
  - Mid-market studios: $8,000–$18,000
  - Premium studios: $15,000–$35,000

RENDIMENSION RECOMMENDATION:

  Large custom home (3,000 sqft, $300K–$500K budget):

  - 2 3D Floor Plans ($850 x 2) = $1,700
  - Video Walkthrough ($2,500 + $2.00 x 3,000) = $8,500
  - Total: $14,700

  Range: $4,500 – $15,000


LUXURY HOME / ESTATE ($500K–$2M)

Standard scope:
  - 6–10 showcase-quality renderings (every room + key details)
  - 3–4 exterior renderings (multiple angles, seasons, times of day)

COMMERCIAL / MULTI-FAMILY ($2M+)
----------------------------------
Standard scope:
  - 8–15 renderings (unit types, amenities, lobby, exterior)
  - 4–6 exterior renderings (street view, aerial, context)
  - 3–5 3D floor plans (unit types + site plan)
  - Full cinematic animation (2–3 minutes)
  - Interactive VR (for sales center)
  - Marketing website with visual assets
  - Seasonal/time-of-day variations

Industry pricing for this scope:
  - Mid-market studios: $25,000–$60,000
  - Premium studios: $50,000–$120,000
  - Full-service packages: $80,000–$200,000+

Note: Commercial developers typically allocate 1–1.5% of projected
sales volume to marketing, with 10–20% of that going to visual content.
For a $10M development, that's $100K–$150K marketing, with $10K–$30K
on visualization. For a $50M project, $50K–$100K+ on visualization.

SECTION 4: THE "SWEET SPOT" — WHERE VIZ COST FEELS JUSTIFIED
============================================================

RULE OF THUMB:
Visualization feels justified when it costs LESS than one bad decision
would cost to fix. This is the "insurance framing."

Examples:
  - $25K kitchen remodel: Changing tile after installation = $3,000–$5,000.
    Spending $1,000–$1,500 on renderings to get it right = obvious value.
    Sweet spot: 4–6% of project budget.

  - $100K full remodel: Moving a wall after framing = $8,000–$15,000.
    Spending $2,500–$4,000 on visualization = cheap insurance.
    Sweet spot: 2.5–4% of project budget.

  - $350K new home: Design changes during construction = $20,000–$50,000.
    Spending $8,000–$12,000 on full visualization = smart investment.
    Sweet spot: 2–3.5% of project budget.

  - $1M luxury home: Any change during construction = $30,000–$100,000+.
    Spending $20,000–$40,000 on comprehensive visualization = standard.
    Sweet spot: 2–4% of project budget.

  - $5M development: Pre-sales acceleration = hundreds of thousands in
    earlier revenue. $50,000–$80,000 visualization = marketing cost.
    Sweet spot: 1–1.5% of development value.

THE PAIN THRESHOLD (where it feels too expensive):
  - Kitchen remodel: Over $3,000 feels expensive (>10% of $30K project)
  - Full remodel: Over $6,000 feels expensive (>5% of $120K project)
  - New home: Over $20,000 needs justification (>5% of $400K project)
  - Luxury: Over $50,000 only for ultra-luxury or full marketing packages
  - Commercial: Budget is flexible if ROI on pre-sales is demonstrated

THE BARGAIN THRESHOLD (where it feels too cheap / low quality):
  - Kitchen remodel: Under $500 signals low quality
  - Full remodel: Under $1,000 signals low quality
  - New home: Under $3,000 signals basic/template work
  - Luxury: Under $10,000 signals insufficient scope
  - Commercial: Under $20,000 signals insufficient scope

SECTION 5: HOW TOP FIRMS STRUCTURE RECOMMENDATIONS
============================================================

THREE MAIN APPROACHES:

1. PERCENTAGE-BASED (least common for small projects)
   - "We recommend allocating 2-5% of your project budget to visualization"
   - Works for large projects where budget is known
   - Fails for homeowners who think in terms of "how many renders" not percentages

2. FIXED TIERS (most common for small-to-mid projects)
   - "Starter Package / Professional Package / Premium Package"
   - Easy to understand, easy to compare
   - Risk: feels arbitrary if tier doesn't match project needs

3. HYBRID (best practice, what Rendimension should use)
   - Ask project type + approximate budget/size
   - Recommend specific deliverables based on what THAT project needs
   - Show a price range that maps to 2-5% of stated budget
   - Let client customize up or down from the recommendation

WHY HYBRID WINS:
  - Feels personalized ("based on what you told us")
  - Anchors to a reasonable number (not random)
  - Gives client control ("want to add/remove?")
  - Avoids sticker shock (range, not single number)
  - Maps to Rendimension's dual-path flow perfectly

SECTION 7: BUDGET-AWARE RECOMMENDATION LOGIC
============================================================

If the estimator ever collects approximate project budget (e.g.,
through a slider or range selector), use this logic:

BUDGET-TO-VIZ MAPPING:

  Under $25K:
    Target viz spend: $500–$1,500
    Max sensible: $2,000
    Suggest: 2 Standard Renderings

  $25K–$50K:
    Target viz spend: $1,000–$2,500
    Max sensible: $3,000
    Suggest: 2–3 Standard or Detailed Renderings

  $50K–$100K:
    Target viz spend: $1,500–$4,000
    Max sensible: $5,000
    Suggest: 3 Detailed Renderings + Floor Plan

  $100K–$250K:
    Target viz spend: $3,000–$8,000
    Max sensible: $12,000
    Suggest: 4–5 Detailed Renderings + Floor Plan + Browser Tour

  $250K–$500K:
    Target viz spend: $5,000–$15,000
    Max sensible: $20,000
    Suggest: 6 Detailed Renderings + 2 Floor Plans + Video Walkthrough

  $500K–$1M:
    Target viz spend: $8,000–$25,000
    Max sensible: $40,000
    Suggest: Full package with Showcase renders + walkthrough + VR

  $1M–$2M:
    Target viz spend: $15,000–$40,000
    Max sensible: $60,000
    Suggest: Full luxury package + VR + cinematic video

  $2M–$5M:
    Target viz spend: $25,000–$60,000
    Max sensible: $100,000
    Suggest: Full marketing package, strategy session

  $5M+:
    Target viz spend: $40,000–$100,000+

NOTE: The estimator should NOT show these percentages to the client.
It should just use them internally to generate the "right-feeling"
recommendation. The client sees deliverables and prices, not ratios.

SECTION 8: IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
============================================================

FOR THE HOMEOWNER PATH:
1. After "What are you working on?" selection, the system already knows
   approximate project complexity.
2. After "What do you want to feel sure about?" the system knows the
   deliverable type.
3. Combine both to generate the recommendation from Section 6.
4. Show as: "Based on projects like yours, here's what we recommend."
5. Always show a range, not a single number.
6. Always offer "Want to adjust? [Customize]" as an escape hatch.

FOR THE PROFESSIONAL PATH:
1. After project type selection, show "Common starting point" from
   Section 6 as a subtle anchor.
2. Pre-check the suggested services in the configurator.
3. Professional can immediately uncheck/modify.
4. Running total updates live.

OPTIONAL ENHANCEMENT — BUDGET SLIDER:
If a future version adds a "What's your approximate project budget?"
slider to the homeowner flow:
1. Use Section 7 mapping to adjust recommendations.
2. If recommended viz exceeds 6% of stated budget, scale down.
3. If recommended viz is under 1.5% of stated budget, suggest upgrade.
4. Never show the percentage calculation to the user.
