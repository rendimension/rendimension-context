## 1. THE VALUE EQUATION — APPLIED TO RENDIMENSION

Hormozi's core formula:

```
Value = (Dream Outcome x Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay x Effort & Sacrifice)
```

To make the offer irresistible: INCREASE the top, DECREASE the bottom.

### Dream Outcome (Increase This)

What clients actually want is NOT renderings. They want:

- **Homeowners:** "I want to know my $200K renovation will look exactly how I imagined before I write the check."
- **Architects:** "I want my client to say yes on the first presentation, not go through 6 rounds of 'can you try something different?'"
- **Developers:** "I want to pre-sell 60% of units before breaking ground so I can secure financing."

**Configurator application:** Every screen should reinforce the DREAM OUTCOME, not the deliverable. The estimate screen should say:

```
WHAT YOU GET:
Not just 3 renderings — a clear picture of your finished project
so you can make decisions with confidence, not guesswork.
```

### Perceived Likelihood of Achievement (Increase This)

The client must believe Rendimension WILL deliver the result. Build this with:

| Trust Signal | Where It Appears | Copy |
|---|---|---|
| Track record | Hero badges | "1,000+ projects delivered" |
| Social proof | Estimate screen | Real testimonial with name, role, result |
| Process clarity | Throughout flow | "Here's exactly what happens after you reserve" |
| Risk reversal | Estimate screen | Guarantee (see Section 3) |
| Portfolio | Hero secondary CTA | "View Our Work" link to portfolio |
| Expert review | Contact screen | "A real person reviews every project" |

### Time Delay (Decrease This)

Clients fear: "How long until I see something?" Reduce perceived time:

| Strategy | Implementation |
|---|---|
| Show delivery timeline | "Typical delivery: 7-10 business days" on estimate screen |
| Rush delivery bonus | "Priority Delivery Guarantee" as a bonus (see Section 2) |
| Instant first step | "Your deposit reserves your spot. We start within 48 hours." |
| Progress updates | "You'll see a preview within 5 business days" |

### Effort & Sacrifice (Decrease This)

Clients fear: "This is going to be a pain to manage." Remove friction:

| Friction Point | Solution |
|---|---|
| "I don't know what I need" | Guided homeowner path recommends for them |
| "Pricing is confusing" | Clear per-image / per-sqft pricing, live total |
| "I have to call someone" | Full estimate in 2 minutes, no phone call required |
| "Payment is complicated" | Multiple payment methods, payment plans (see Section 5) |
| "I have to explain my project 10 times" | One brief, one point of contact, one process |
| "Revisions will be a nightmare" | Include revision rounds in every package |

## 3. RISK REVERSAL STRATEGIES

### What the Industry Does

Most visualization studios offer NO guarantee. This is Rendimension's opportunity to stand out.

- **Crowdspring** offers 100% money back within 30 days if unsatisfied
- **Renderby** charges a 17.5% "peace of mind fee" that includes a satisfaction guarantee
- **LogoBee** offers full satisfaction or money back minus a 3.5% admin fee
- **Logo Design Team** offers open-ended "if you aren't happy, tell us and we'll take care of you"

### Recommended Guarantee Structure for Rendimension

#### Option A: "The Clarity Guarantee" (Recommended)

```
THE CLARITY GUARANTEE

If your first round of renderings doesn't match the brief we agreed on,
we'll revise them until they do — at no extra cost.

If after two revision rounds you're still not satisfied,
we'll refund your deposit in full. No questions, no hassle.

We've delivered 1,000+ projects. We know what we're doing.
But we want YOU to feel sure before you commit.
```

**Why this works:**
- Named guarantee (not just "money back guarantee")
- Specific conditions (must not match the agreed brief)
- Reasonable scope (two revision rounds, then refund deposit)
- Confidence language ("We know what we're doing")
- Ties back to brand: "feel sure"

#### Option B: "The 100% Fit Guarantee" (Aggressive)

```
THE 100% FIT GUARANTEE

Love your renderings or we redo them free.

Here's how it works:
1. We produce your renderings based on the approved brief
2. You review them
3. If anything doesn't match what we agreed on, we fix it — free
4. If after revisions you're genuinely unsatisfied, we refund your deposit

No fine print. No awkward conversations.
Your project is too important for "close enough."
```

#### Option C: "The Results Guarantee" (For Developer/Investor Packages)

```
THE PRE-SALE GUARANTEE

Our renderings are designed to help you sell.

If you use our visualization package for a development project
and don't receive a single qualified inquiry within 60 days
of launching your marketing materials, we'll produce
one additional rendering at no charge.

Because if your project isn't getting attention,
we want to help fix that.
```

### Where to Display the Guarantee

- **Estimate screen:** Below the itemized total, above the CTA buttons
- **As a bonus line item:** Listed in the value stack with a perceived value ($500-$1,000)
- **Trust badge:** Small shield icon + "Clarity Guarantee" on the contact and estimate screens

## 4. ETHICAL SCARCITY AND URGENCY

### Hormozi's Three Scarcity Methods

1. **Total Business Cap:** "We only take 12 projects per month"
2. **Growth Rate Cap:** "We accept 3 new clients per week"
3. **Cohort Cap:** "This month's project slots are limited to 8"

### Recommended for Rendimension

#### Strategy: Monthly Project Slots (Real Capacity Constraint)

If Rendimension genuinely has a capacity limit (which every studio does), use it honestly.

**Configurator implementation — Estimate screen:**

```
PROJECT AVAILABILITY

We take on [X] projects per month to maintain quality.
Currently: [Y] slots remaining for [Month].

Your deposit reserves your spot in our production schedule.
```

**Rules:**
- Only show this if it's TRUE. Update monthly.
- Never show "1 slot left" if there are 5. Honesty is the scarcity.
- If fully booked, show: "Our [Month] schedule is full. Reserve now for [Next Month] delivery."

#### Strategy: Estimate Validity Window

```
This estimate is valid for 14 days.
After that, pricing and availability may change.
```

This is standard business practice, not fake urgency. Pricing does change. Availability does shift.

#### Strategy: Seasonal Pricing (Optional, Use Sparingly)

```
[Q1 only] New Year Project Launch — Reserve before January 31
and lock in 2025 pricing for projects starting in Q1.
```

Only use this during genuine pricing transitions or slow seasons.

### What NOT to Do

- No countdown timers
- No "ONLY 2 LEFT!" pop-ups
- No fake "this offer expires in 24 hours"
- No "prices going up soon" without an actual price increase scheduled

This aligns with the existing CLAUDE.md rule: "No fake urgency. No countdown timers, no 'SAVE X% TODAY', no promo language."

## 5. PAYMENT FLEXIBILITY AS A BENEFIT

### The Problem

A $15,000 project quote triggers sticker shock. The client likes the work, wants to proceed, but the number feels heavy. Payment flexibility removes that barrier.

### Framing Payment Options as Benefits (Not Just Logistics)

**Bad (feature):** "We accept credit cards, debit, ACH, wire, and checks."

**Good (benefit):**

```
PAY THE WAY THAT WORKS FOR YOU

Every project, every budget — we make it easy.

Credit or debit card
Bank transfer (ACH)
Wire transfer
Check
Payment plans available for projects over $5,000

Your deposit is applied to your total.
The rest is due before final delivery.
```

### Payment Plan Structure for the Configurator

| Project Total | Payment Option | Monthly Equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Under $2,000 | Full payment or 50/50 | — |
| $2,000 – $5,000 | 50% upfront / 50% on delivery | — |
| $5,000 – $10,000 | 3 monthly payments | Show: "as low as $X/month" |
| $10,000 – $25,000 | 4 monthly payments | Show: "as low as $X/month" |
| $25,000+ | Custom payment schedule | "Let's build a payment plan that works for you" |

### "As Low As" Display in the Configurator

On the estimate screen, below the total:

```
ESTIMATED TOTAL: $9,600

Or as low as $3,200/month for 3 months
[See payment options]
```

This reduces perceived sacrifice (bottom of the Value Equation).

### Copy for Payment Section

```
FLEXIBLE PAYMENT OPTIONS

We never want budget timing to get in the way of a great project.

For projects over $5,000, we offer simple monthly payment plans.
No interest. No credit checks. Just a schedule that works for you.

All major payment methods accepted:
Visa / Mastercard / Amex / Bank Transfer / Wire / Check
```

## 7. ANCHOR AND DISCOUNT — CONFIGURATOR DISPLAY PATTERN

### The Psychology

The first number a person sees becomes their anchor. If they see $22,800 in total value first, then $14,900 as the price, $14,900 FEELS like a deal — even though it's a large number.

### How to Display It in the Configurator

**Step 1: Itemize everything with individual values**

Each line item gets its own value. Each bonus gets its own value. This makes the stack tangible.

**Step 2: Show "Total Value" prominently**

```
TOTAL VALUE: $22,800
```

Use normal weight, standard size. This is the anchor.

**Step 3: Show "Your Price" below it**

```
YOUR PRICE: $14,900
```

Use bold weight, slightly larger. This is what they focus on.

**Step 4: Do NOT cross out the total value**

Crossing out prices looks like a clearance sale. For a premium studio, instead:

```
TOTAL VALUE:  $22,800
YOUR PRICE:   $14,900

You're receiving $7,900 in bonuses — included at no extra cost.
```

This frames bonuses as a gift, not a discount. It says "we're generous" not "we're desperate."

**Step 5: Show monthly equivalent (for projects over $5,000)**

```
Or $3,725/month × 4 months — no interest
```

This creates a SECOND anchor. Now the brain is comparing $14,900 to $3,725/month, and $3,725/month feels very manageable for a business.

### Implementation in the Configurator

On the estimate screen (Screen 5/6/7 depending on path), the layout should be:

```
YOUR PROJECT ESTIMATE
─────────────────────────────────────

SERVICES
  8 Showcase Renderings              $9,600
  Browser Virtual Tour (3,000 sqft)  $7,000
  2 × 3D Floor Plans                $1,700
                                    ────────
  Services subtotal                 $18,300

INCLUDED WITH YOUR PROJECT
  Design Alignment Session            $250
  Confidence Guarantee (2 rounds)   $1,000
  Material Match Blueprint            $500
  Contractor-Ready File Kit           $400
  Instant Marketing Kit               $300
  Lighting Study                      $350
  Boardroom-Ready Deck                $800
  Priority Delivery Guarantee         $750
  Multi-Device Preview Files          $150
                                    ────────
  Bonuses included                  $4,500

─────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL VALUE                        $22,800
YOUR PRICE                         $14,900
─────────────────────────────────────

$7,900 in bonuses — included with your project.

Or $3,725/month × 4 months

Typical delivery: 14-18 business days
Covered by The Clarity Guarantee
```

### Key Design Rules

1. **Bonuses appear as their own section** — "INCLUDED WITH YOUR PROJECT" — not mixed into the service lines
2. **Each bonus has a dollar value** — this makes the stack feel real and quantifiable
3. **"Total Value" appears before "Your Price"** — anchor first, price second
4. **Never use strikethrough on the total value** — that's discount retail behavior
5. **The word "bonuses" appears in the savings line** — reinforces that these are extras
6. **Monthly payment appears as the last price reference** — smallest number stays in memory

## 8. REAL EXAMPLES FROM CREATIVE/DESIGN SERVICES

### Studios with Compelling Guarantee Language

| Studio | Guarantee | What Rendimension Can Learn |
|---|---|---|
| Crowdspring | "100% money back within 30 days" | Absolute simplicity. No conditions visible upfront. |
| Renderby | 17.5% "peace of mind fee" with satisfaction guarantee | Interesting model — charge MORE for the guarantee as a premium. |
| ZOA Studio | "Impression Package" — 15 renders in 5 days, one briefing | Package as a SYSTEM, not a list of deliverables. |
| CYLIND Studio | "Scope, budget, and completion defined upfront" | Transparency IS the offer. No surprises = no anxiety. |
| Rapid Renders | Express delivery for launches, competitions, investor presentations | Urgency-based packaging tied to client deadlines. |

### Pattern: The Best Studios Sell Systems, Not Services

The most successful offers in creative services aren't "buy 5 renderings." They are:

- **"The Launch System"** — everything you need to launch your development
- **"The Approval Package"** — everything you need to get your design approved
- **"The Pre-Sale Engine"** — everything you need to pre-sell before construction

Rendimension should name packages after the CLIENT'S GOAL, not the deliverable.
