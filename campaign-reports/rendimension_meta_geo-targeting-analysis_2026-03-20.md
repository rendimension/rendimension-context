## 2. META ADS REALITY CHECK AT $9/DAY

### The Math
- Average CPL for Meta Lead Ads in 2026: **$27.66**
- At $9/day: you get roughly **2-3 leads per week** (optimistic)
- At $270/month: expect **8-12 leads per month** if creative is strong
- Meta's learning phase needs **50 conversions in 7 days** — at $9/day, you will NEVER exit the learning phase normally

### What This Means
Your campaign will permanently live in "learning limited" status. This is not a dealbreaker — many small businesses run profitably in learning limited. But it means:
- Performance will be more volatile day-to-day
- You need to CONSOLIDATE everything into **1 campaign, 1 ad set** (no splitting)
- Every edit resets learning — so set it and leave it for at least 7 days

### Optimal Audience Size at $9/Day
- Meta recommends **10,000–50,000** for lead gen conversion campaigns
- For $9/day budget, broader is better — the algorithm needs room to find cheap impressions
- Ideal range: **500,000 to 2,000,000** estimated reach (this gives Meta enough room to optimize without CPM spikes from narrow audiences)

## 3. THE THREE OPTIONS — COMPARED

### OPTION A: National US (Current Setup)
- **Estimated audience:** 5,000,000+ (with interest targeting) / 200M+ (full broad)
- **Pros:** Maximum algorithm freedom. Lowest CPMs. Meta finds the cheapest leads wherever they are.
- **Cons:** Leads could come from anywhere — including markets with no development activity. Risk of low-quality leads from small towns with no real projects.
- **Verdict:** Too broad for a specialized B2B service at this budget.

### OPTION B: Top 20 Development Metros
Houston, DFW, Phoenix, Atlanta, Charlotte, Orlando, Austin, NYC, LA, Nashville, Tampa, Miami, San Antonio, Denver, Raleigh, Jacksonville, San Diego, Chicago, Seattle, Las Vegas
- **Estimated audience:** 2,000,000–4,000,000 (with age 40+ and broad interest signals)
- **Pros:** Covers 80%+ of all US development activity. Large enough for Meta to optimize. Every lead likely works in or near active development.
- **Cons:** Still fairly wide — 20 metros is a lot of ground for $9/day.
- **Verdict:** Good balance, but may be slightly too spread out for the budget.

### OPTION C: Top 8-10 Development + Architecture Metros (RECOMMENDED)
**Miami, Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, Austin, Phoenix, Atlanta, Orlando-Tampa corridor, Nashville**
- **Estimated audience:** 1,000,000–2,500,000 (with age 40+)
- **Pros:**
  - Covers the highest-activity development markets
  - Miami is your home market with rising AIA billings
  - Texas has 4 of the top 10 construction metros
  - Florida + Southeast are the fastest-growing construction regions
  - Audience size is in the sweet spot for $9/day
  - Every lead will be in an active development market
- **Cons:** Excludes NYC, LA, San Francisco (high architect density but also highest ad costs — CPMs in NYC/LA run 30-50% higher)
- **Verdict:** BEST option for this budget.

## 4. SHOULD YOU RESTRICT GEOGRAPHY WITH ADVANTAGE+ ON?

### The Short Answer: YES, with a light touch.

Here's why:

**Without geo restriction (full broad national):**
- Meta will spend your $9 finding the cheapest impressions anywhere in the US
- Cheapest impressions ≠ best leads
- You'll get leads from rural areas or markets with no development activity
- Algorithm optimizes for FORM SUBMISSIONS, not for LEAD QUALITY

**With geo restriction (Top 8-10 metros):**
- You guarantee every dollar is spent in markets where developers and architects actually work
- Audience is still large enough (1M-2.5M) for Meta's AI to optimize within
- Broad audience CPM ($8-$10) vs narrow interest CPM ($10-$15) — by keeping targeting broad WITHIN the geos, you get the best of both worlds
- You pre-filter geography so the algorithm only needs to find the RIGHT PEOPLE, not the right places

**Meta's own position:** Advantage+ works best with broad targeting, yes — but "broad" means don't over-layer interests and demographics. Geographic filtering is the ONE constraint that makes sense for B2B services because it pre-qualifies the market without limiting the algorithm's ability to find converters.

## 5. FINAL RECOMMENDATION

### Target These 10 Metros (Drop-Pin + Radius in Ads Manager)

| Metro | Why |
|-------|-----|
| **Miami-Fort Lauderdale, FL** | Home market. Only US region with rising AIA billings. Luxury condo boom. |
| **Houston, TX** | #1 in construction permits nationally. 24,750 SFH permits. |
| **Dallas-Fort Worth, TX** | #2 in permits. $89.7B annual commercial construction. |
| **Austin, TX** | #1 city-level for apartment construction (15,195 units). Tech-driven growth. |
| **Phoenix-Mesa, AZ** | #3 in permits. Massive residential expansion. |
| **Atlanta, GA** | #4 in permits. Southeast logistics/tech hub. |
| **Orlando, FL** | #6 in permits. Florida corridor growth. |
| **Tampa-St. Pete, FL** | Fastest-growing SE construction market. Major redevelopment projects. |
| **Nashville, TN** | #10 in permits. Hot market for architects. |
| **Charlotte, NC** | #5 in permits. Banking/finance driving luxury development. |

### Campaign Structure
- **1 campaign** (Advantage+ Leads or standard Lead Gen with CBO)
- **1 ad set** (consolidate all budget into one)
- **Geographic targeting:** These 10 metros, 25-mile radius each
- **Age:** 35+ (don't restrict to 40+ — let Meta optimize within a slightly broader range)
- **Interests:** NONE — let Advantage+ find the right people based on creative signals
- **Budget:** $9/day at the campaign level
- **Creative:** 3-6 variants with clear "developer/architect" language in the hook so Meta's AI learns WHO engages

### Why NOT Include NYC, LA, San Francisco?
- CPMs in these markets are 30-50% higher ($15-$25 vs $8-$12 in Sun Belt)
- At $9/day, expensive metros would eat your entire budget reaching fewer people
- The Sun Belt metros have MORE active development AND cheaper ad costs
- When you scale to $20-30/day, THEN add NYC and LA

### Expected Results at $9/Day with This Targeting
- **CPM estimate:** $10-$15 (B2B audience in development metros)
- **Estimated daily reach:** 600-900 people
- **CTR target:** 1-2%
- **Monthly impressions:** ~18,000-27,000
- **Monthly leads (realistic):** 6-12
- **Estimated CPL:** $22-$45

### When to Expand
- If CPL stays under $40 for 2+ weeks → add NYC metro
- If CPL stays under $30 for 2+ weeks → add LA + San Francisco
- Scale budget by 20% max at a time, wait 3-4 days between increases

## 6. BOTTOM LINE FOR HUGO

**Current setup (National US broad) is wasting money.** At $9/day, you can't afford to show ads in Omaha, Nebraska to someone who will never need 3D renders.

**The move:** Target the 10 metros where buildings are actually being built. Your $9 goes further in Houston ($8 CPM) than in Manhattan ($20 CPM), and the developers in Houston are building MORE than developers in Manhattan right now.

Think of it this way: you're not restricting the audience — you're concentrating your firepower where the buyers are.

**One campaign. One ad set. Ten metros. Let the creative do the qualifying.**
