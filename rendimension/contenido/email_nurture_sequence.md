## IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

**Setup in Your Email Platform (HubSpot, ActiveCampaign, Klaviyo, etc.):**

1. **Trigger:** New lead inquiry form submission
2. **Sequence:**
   - Email 1: Immediate send
   - Email 2: +2 days
   - Email 3: +4 days
   - Email 4: +7 days
   - Email 5: +14 days

3. **Conditional Logic:**
   - If lead engages (clicks, replies): Stop sequence and transfer to manual sales follow-up
   - If project type is "already decided": Skip to Email 4 (case study)
   - If lead requests contact: Trigger CRM task for sales team immediately

4. **Tracking:**
   - Monitor open rates per email (target: 25-35% for B2B architecture/real estate)
   - Track click-through rates on portfolio and call booking links
   - Measure conversion to scheduled calls (target: 8-12% of sequences sent)

5. **Personalization Tokens:**
   - [First Name]
   - [Project Type] (if captured in form)
   - [Company Name] (if B2B inquiry)

6. **Sender:** Best practice—send from the most relevant team member (account manager or principal, not noreply@)

**A/B Testing Recommendations:**

- Test subject line options across first 100 leads, then standardize
- Test CTA button text vs. text link performance
- Measure Day 2 vs. Day 3 send time for Email 2
- Split test "schedule call" vs. "reply with details" CTAs
