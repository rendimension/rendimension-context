# Reflex Pricing Market Research Report
**Platform:** Reflex (reflexsystem.com) — AI + VR Sales Training for Automotive Dealerships
**Research Date:** 2026-03-18
**Prepared for:** Hugo Ramirez
**Pricing Under Review:** $35,000 setup fee + $3,500/month per dealership rooftop

---

## Executive Summary

Reflex's proposed pricing is aggressive but defensible. The $35,000 setup fee sits at the bottom of the VR enterprise implementation range ($40,000–$150,000+ for comparable platforms), which positions it as accessible relative to pure VR vendors. The $3,500/month retainer ($42,000/year) is well within the spending envelope of a dealership that already burns $450,000/year on turnover, $543,000/year on advertising, and $30,000–$80,000/year on its technology stack. The critical friction point is not price — it is proof. Dealer Principals will not write a $35,000 check without a concrete ROI case expressed in units sold or gross profit gained. The pitch must speak in dollars, not training concepts.

Total annual cost to a dealership (setup amortized over 2 years + monthly): **$59,500 in Year 1, $42,000 in Year 2+.**

Even at a conservative 5% improvement (6 extra units/month): $13,482 additional gross vs. $3,500/month cost. The pitch math is unambiguous.

## Section 4: Friction Analysis for $35,000 Setup + $3,500/Month

### 4A. Who Approves This Decision

| Role | Budget Authority | What They Care About | Friction Level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dealer Principal / Owner** | Full authority for any purchase | Net profit, ROI, avoiding bad bets, protecting cash | HIGH — this is an unusual capital outlay |
| **General Manager** | Typically up to $5,000–$10,000 independently | Operations efficiency, hitting monthly targets | MEDIUM — will advocate up if convinced |
| **Sales Manager** | Minimal independent authority (under $2,000 typically) | Coaching tools, reducing their roleplay burden | LOW — wants the tool, no checkbook |
| **Automotive Group VP** | Full authority across portfolio | Standardization, scalability, measuring ROI by rooftop | HIGH for initial deal; LOW for renewals once proven |

**Practical reality:** A $35,000 check requires the Dealer Principal's signature in virtually all single-point and small group scenarios. General Managers at larger groups may have authority up to $10,000–$25,000. Any deal above that amount escalates to ownership. This is not an obstacle — it is a design constraint. Reflex's LinkedIn outreach must target Dealer Principals and Group VPs directly, not just GMs or Sales Managers.

---

### 4B. Typical Contract Length for Similar Tools

| Tool Type | Typical Contract Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Enterprise SaaS (B2B) | 12 months as baseline; 24–36 months for complex/high-value | SaaS Capital / Baremetrics data |
| DMS platforms (CDK, Reynolds) | 5–7 year contracts common | Very sticky; high switching cost |
| CRM platforms | 12–24 months | Renewal common; switching costly |
| Training platforms | 12 months typical; 24 months with setup fee justification | NCM, JVTN model |
| VR training platforms | 12–24 months minimum | Hardware logistics require longer commitment |

**Recommendation for Reflex:** A 12-month minimum commitment is standard and expected. A 24-month contract with a discounted setup fee ($35K down to $28K if signing 24 months) creates a compelling incentive and improves lifetime value. Monthly-only pricing without a setup fee could work at higher monthly cost ($5,500–$6,000/month) for dealers who resist upfront capital.

| Benchmark | Amount | vs. Reflex $35K Setup |
|---|---|---|
| Annual NCM in-dealership training (8 days) | $36,000 | Reflex is comparable but 365-day |
| One bad salesperson hire and fire cycle | $97,690 | Reflex costs less than half of one turnover |
| Annual dealership ad spend | $543,539 | Reflex = 6.4% of ad budget for sales layer |
| Annual technology stack | $30,000–$80,000 | Reflex fits within existing tech envelope |
| Annual dealership net profit (2024) | $4.0–$4.5M | $35K setup = 0.8% of annual net profit |
| Monthly cost vs. gross on 2 incremental units | $3,500 vs. $4,494+ | One salesperson sells 2 extra units per month = platform pays for itself |

**Verdict:** The $35,000 setup is not expensive relative to the business context. It is expensive relative to the buyer's reference point (they are trained to think in $500–$2,000 software fees). The ad campaign must reset the reference point before presenting price.

## Section 5: Strategic Pricing Recommendations

### 5A. Price Positioning Options

| Tier | Structure | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | $35,000 setup + $3,500/month | Single-point dealers with clear ROI conversation |
| **Annual Prepay** | $35,000 setup + $37,000/year (save $5,000 vs. monthly) | Cash-rich dealers who think annually |
| **Commitment Discount** | $28,000 setup + $3,500/month on 24-month contract | Dealers hesitant on upfront; want better deal |
| **Group Deal** | $35,000 setup per rooftop; $2,800/month per rooftop at 3+ rooftops | Dealer groups; volume discount for group VP buy-in |
| **Pilot** | $15,000 setup (one rooftop, 90 days) | Skeptics who need proof before full commitment |

### 5B. Comparable Annual Cost to Dealership

**Year 1 Total:** $35,000 + ($3,500 x 12) = **$77,000**
**Year 2+ Annual:** $3,500 x 12 = **$42,000**
**2-Year Total:** **$119,000**

Context: A dealership spending $543,000/year on advertising and $450,000/year on turnover costs views $77,000 in Year 1 as less than 5% of their combined spend on two known budget lines. The number is not the problem. The proof is the problem.
