## DESIGN SYSTEM REFERENCE (All Assets)

Before any individual brief, these rules apply to every asset in this package without exception.

**Primary Color Palette**
- Background base: `#0D0D0D` (near-black, not pure black — adds depth)
- Deep charcoal panels: `#1A1A2E`
- Electric blue accent: `#0078FF`
- Secondary blue (highlights, underlines): `#3FA9F5`
- White body text: `#FFFFFF`
- Light gray supporting text: `#B0B0B0`
- Gold accent (use sparingly, financial/premium contexts only): `#C9A84C`

**Typography Rules**
- Headline: Bold sans-serif. Recommended: Inter Bold, Montserrat ExtraBold, or Bebas Neue for maximum weight. Minimum 72px at 1080x1080 production size.
- Subheadline: Inter SemiBold or Montserrat SemiBold. 36-48px.
- Body/descriptor text: Inter Regular or Montserrat Regular. 24-28px.
- All type on dark backgrounds: `#FFFFFF` default; blue `#0078FF` for emphasis words only.
- Letter-spacing on headlines: -0.02em (tighter than default, feels premium).

**Logo Placement**
- Always bottom-right corner.
- Minimum clear space: 32px from edge.
- Use white version of logo on dark backgrounds.
- Never scale logo larger than 10% of total canvas width.
- Do not place logo on busy image areas — use a semi-transparent dark overlay zone if needed.

**What to Avoid (Non-Negotiable)**
- No stock photos of smiling salespeople in suits.
- No floating VR headsets on white backgrounds.
- No bright/pastel color schemes.
- No generic "business meeting" photography.
- No light backgrounds unless specifically noted as a variant test.
- No cluttered text — maximum 2 text elements per major visual zone.

**Production Tool:** Canva
**Reason:** This is a premium-feeling document cover that requires precise typography layout and dark background control — Canva handles this efficiently with custom brand colors and text hierarchy. Upgrade to 21ST if a more cinematic background image is required.

---

### 1. Visual Concept

The cover reads as a serious industry intelligence report, not a marketing brochure. Think McKinsey automotive practice meets a premium trade journal — dark, authoritative, data-forward.

**Layout structure (top to bottom, left-right):**

- **Background:** Full-bleed dark image (see AI prompt below) occupying the bottom 60% of the canvas. The image is desaturated or color-graded with a deep blue-black filter so it does not compete with the text.
- **Top section (40% of canvas height):** Solid `#0D0D0D` background panel that bleeds to the edges. No gradient — a clean horizontal line separates it from the image below. This top panel is where all primary text lives.
- **Thin electric blue rule:** A 3px horizontal line at `#0078FF` runs the full width directly below the top text panel, creating a visual separator that signals precision and professionalism.
- **Bottom-right corner:** Reflex logo in white, positioned above a semi-transparent dark strip if the background image is busy at that corner.

**Overall feel:** If you removed all the text, this would still look like the cover of a premium automotive industry report. The design never looks like a social media ad.

### 4. AI Image Prompt (Midjourney / DALL-E 3)

Paste this prompt exactly:

```
Interior of a modern automotive dealership showroom at dusk, shot from a low angle looking across the sales floor toward a row of new vehicles under showroom lighting. The floor is polished concrete or dark tile, reflecting vehicle silhouettes. Dramatic chiaroscuro lighting — bright overhead spotlights on vehicles, deep shadows between them. ---
```

### 5. Canva Brief (Step-by-Step for Designer)

1. Create new Canva design at 1080 x 1080px.
2. Set page background to `#0D0D0D`.
3. Upload the AI-generated showroom image. Place it as the background layer. Resize so it fills the bottom 55% of the canvas (from approximately y=500 to y=1080).
4. Add a black rectangle (opacity 65%) over the full canvas as a darkening overlay. Send to back but above the image layer.
5. Add a solid rectangle (`#0D0D0D`, no opacity) at the top of the canvas from y=0 to y=420, full width. This creates the clean top panel.
6. Add a thin horizontal rectangle (3px height, full width, `#0078FF`) at y=422. This is the blue rule separator.
7. **Type Layer 1 (label):** Add text at x=48, y=60. Text: "GENERAL MANAGER BRIEFING — 2026". Font: Inter Medium. Size: 18px. Color: `#B0B0B0`. Letter spacing: 0.15em.
8. **Type Layer 2 (main headline):** Add text block at x=48, y=100. Text: "5 OBJECTIONS / KILLING YOUR / CLOSING RATE" (line break after each phrase). Font: Montserrat ExtraBold. Size: 96px. Color: `#FFFFFF`. Line height: 1.05. The word "KILLING" can be in `#0078FF` for emphasis on one variant — test both.
9. **Type Layer 3 (subheadline):** Add text at x=48, y=360. Text: "And What VR Training Does About It". Font: Inter Regular. Size: 30px. Color: `#B0B0B0`.
10. **Bottom strip:** Add thin rectangle at y=990 to y=1080, full width, `#1A1A2E`, opacity 90%.
11. Inside bottom strip, add text left-aligned: "Free Guide — reflexsystem.com". Font: Inter Regular. Size: 22px. Color: `#3FA9F5`.
12. **Logo:** Place Reflex white logo at bottom-right of the bottom strip. Scale to approx 120px wide. Maintain proportions.
13. Export as PNG (for cover thumbnail) and PDF (for full document upload).

---

### 6. Visual Hierarchy (What the Eye Reads, In Order)

1. **First (0.3 seconds):** "5 OBJECTIONS" — the number and the word in combination create immediate pattern recognition for a GM who deals with objections daily.
2. **Second (0.5-1 second):** "KILLING YOUR CLOSING RATE" — completes the threat. The GM reads this as a statement about their own store.
3. **Third (1-2 seconds):** "And What VR Training Does About It" — introduces the solution without over-promising.
4. **Fourth:** The dealership background image registers — contextual credibility signal. This is not a tech ad, it is an automotive industry ad.
5. **Fifth:** The URL and logo — identifies the source, signals legitimacy.

### Variant 1 — Closing Rate Angle

**Ad Name:** `reflex_linkedin_closingrate_inmail_header_A`
**Paired InMail copy:** Variant A — closing rate ROI angle (to Dealer Principals)

---

**1. Visual Concept**

Dark background (`#0D0D0D`). A single bold data statement in the center. Below the number, a thin electric blue underline. A very faint dealership environment image in the background at low opacity (10-15%) gives context without visual noise. The overall aesthetic reads: financial data point, not marketing banner.

---

**2. Text on Image (Exact Words)**

**Large number (center, dominant):**
`+3%`

**Below the number (small, light gray):**
`closing rate = $15,000/mo additional gross`

**Thin rule:** `#0078FF`, 40px wide, centered, directly below the "+3%" text.

**Font sizing at 300x250px:**
- "+3%": Montserrat ExtraBold, 72px, `#FFFFFF`
- Subtext: Inter Regular, 14px, `#B0B0B0`
- No logo at this size — it would compete with the data statement. Logo is unnecessary; the InMail itself carries the Reflex sender identity.

---

**3. AI Image Prompt (Midjourney / DALL-E 3)**

```
Extreme close-up of a car dealership sales dashboard or CRM screen showing sales performance metrics — blurred enough to be abstract but with the visual language of data rows and percentages. Deep navy and charcoal color grade. Screen glow providing the only light source. No faces, no branding. Photorealistic, very dark, cinematic. Square 1:1 aspect ratio.
```

---

**4. Canva Brief (Step-by-Step)**

3. Upload AI background at full canvas size, opacity 12%.
4. **Main number:** "+3%" centered at canvas center (x=150, y=95 approximately). Montserrat ExtraBold, 72px, `#FFFFFF`.

**5. Visual Hierarchy**

1. **First:** "+3%" — number and positive valence signal immediately. A DP reads this and asks: 3% what?

### Variant 2 — New Hire Ramp Angle

**Ad Name:** `reflex_linkedin_newhireramp_inmail_header_B`
**Paired InMail copy:** Variant B — new hire ramp time angle (to Dealer Principals)

---

**1. Visual Concept**

Same dark base as Variant 1. This time the dominant visual element is a timeline or countdown — not a percentage, but a comparison of two numbers: "90 days" crossed out in light red or gray, and "30 days" in white. This creates a before/after compression that reads instantly as: ramp time cut by two-thirds.

---

**2. Text on Image (Exact Words)**

**Crossed-out text (large, `#888888` with strikethrough styling):**
`90 days`

**Arrow or vs. divider (small, `#0078FF`):**
`→`

**New number (large, white, dominant):**
`30 days`

**Below, small label:**
`new hire to full production`

**Font sizing at 300x250px:**
- "90 days": Montserrat ExtraBold, 48px, `#777777`, with a horizontal line drawn through it.
- Arrow "→": Montserrat Bold, 36px, `#0078FF`, centered between the two numbers.
- "30 days": Montserrat ExtraBold, 64px, `#FFFFFF` (larger than "90 days" to signal the outcome).
- Label: Inter Regular, 13px, `#B0B0B0`.

---

**3. AI Image Prompt (Midjourney / DALL-E 3)**

```
Abstract calendar or timeline metaphor — blurred background showing faint calendar grid lines or a horizontal progress bar with markers, very dark navy and charcoal, almost invisible. The visual texture of "time passing" without literal clock imagery. No faces, no cars, no people. Photorealistic abstract, square 1:1 aspect ratio.
```

---

**4. Canva Brief (Step-by-Step)**

1. Create new Canva design at 300 x 250px. Background: `#0D0D0D`.
2. Upload AI background full canvas, opacity 10%.
3. **"90 days" text:** Centered, y=75. Montserrat ExtraBold 46px `#777777`.
4. **Strikethrough:** Add a 2px horizontal rectangle `#777777` across the center of "90 days" text.
5. **Arrow:** Centered, y=130. Montserrat Bold 36px `#0078FF`. Single character "→".
6. **"30 days":** Centered, y=165. Montserrat ExtraBold 62px `#FFFFFF`.
7. **Label:** Centered, y=215. Inter Regular 13px `#B0B0B0`. "new hire to full production".
8. No logo.
9. Export PNG at 300x250px.

---

**5. Visual Hierarchy**

1. **First:** The contrast between "90" crossed out and "30" in white — before/after framing is one of the fastest-reading visual patterns in advertising.
2. **Second:** The strikethrough signals that the longer number is the problem being solved.
3. **Third:** "new hire to full production" — context for what those numbers mean.
