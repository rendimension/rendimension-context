## 2. THE BIG OPPORTUNITY

- **The category is unclaimed.** The LinkedIn Ad Library has zero VR training ads targeting automotive General Managers or Dealer Principals. Every traditional competitor (Joe Verde, NCM, NADA, ATN) is running passive video content. Nobody owns the "practice" positioning. First mover here means category ownership, not just market share.

- **The timing is structural, not tactical.** Dealerships are under margin pressure. OEMs are pulling back incentive money. GMs need their floor teams closing at a higher rate NOW — not in a quarter. VR training that delivers measurable closing rate improvement is a direct answer to an active P&L problem, not a nice-to-have.

- **The audience is on LinkedIn and reachable.** Estimated 35,000-65,000 General Managers, Dealer Principals, and Sales Managers in the US are reachable with LinkedIn's automotive industry filter. This is a small, high-value audience. A $5,000/month budget can touch every relevant decision maker in the country inside 60 days.

## 3. DECISION MATRIX — WHAT TO ADVERTISE

| Service / Angle | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Objection Handling VR | GO — Lead with this | Highest-frequency pain point on the floor. GMs feel this every week. Easiest to demonstrate ROI. |
| Closing Scenario VR | GO — Second priority | Directly tied to closing rate, which is the metric GMs and DPs track obsessively. |

## 4. CAMPAIGN LAUNCH PLAN

### PHASE 1 — Launch (Month 1-2)

**Three campaigns running simultaneously:**

**Campaign 1 — Consequence-Free Practice (GMs + DPs)**
- Audience: General Managers, Automotive GMs, Dealer Principals, Dealership Owners
- Industry filter: Automotive | Company size: 11-500 employees | Geo: United States
- Estimated reach: 35,000-65,000 people
- Format: Document Ad (3 variants — pain hook / stat hook / question hook)
- Offer: Free Benchmark Report — "How does your team's closing rate compare to top-10% dealerships?"
- Why document ads: They stop the scroll, feel like value not an ad, and LinkedIn favors them in the feed
- Budget allocation: 50% of total monthly budget
- Naming: `reflex_linkedin_leadgen_gmdp_20260318`

**Campaign 2 — ROI Math (Dealer Principals)**
- Audience: Dealer Principals, Dealership Owners only (tightest filter)
- Format: Document Ad (3 variants — pain hook / stat hook / FOMO hook)
- Offer: Free Objection Audit — 20-minute call, written output showing exactly where floor reps are losing deals
- Why separate: DPs think in P&L language. The message is different: "What is one extra closed deal per week worth to your store?" not "train your team."
- Budget allocation: 30% of total monthly budget
- Naming: `reflex_linkedin_leadgen_dealerprincipals_20260318`

**Campaign 3 — Sales Manager Liberation (Sales Managers)**
- Audience: Sales Managers, Automotive Sales Managers
- Format: Single Image Ad (3 variants testing different pain angles)
- Offer: Free Benchmark Report (same asset as Campaign 1 — no new creative needed)
- Why include: Sales Managers are internal champions. If one loves Reflex, they sell it upward to the GM. Low cost, high leverage.
- Budget allocation: 20% of total monthly budget
- Naming: `reflex_linkedin_leadgen_salesmanagers_20260318`

**Phase 1 Offers in play:**
- Offer A — Free Benchmark Report (low-friction, awareness stage, 3-field form)
- Offer B — Free Objection Audit, 20-minute call with written output (consideration stage, high-value leads)

### PHASE 2 — Scale (Month 3-4)

Based on what wins in Phase 1:

- **If Document Ads win:** Build 3 new document variants on the winning hook type. Do not change the hook — change the visual inside the document.
- **If pain-hook wins over stat-hook:** Rewrite the stat and FOMO variants as pain variants. Kill what's losing.
- **ABM Play — Enterprise Groups:** Build a separate campaign targeting employees at AutoNation, Lithia, Asbury, Hendrick, Penske, Group 1, and Sonic Automotive. These are the largest dealer groups in the US. One contract here equals 50+ rooftops. Use LinkedIn's company name targeting to reach their VP-level operators directly.
- **Add Offer C:** The 30-Day Pilot (install Reflex in their store, measure their metrics, no commitment). This is a decision-stage offer for leads who engaged in Phase 1 but did not book a call.
- **Add F&I Managers** as a new audience segment with their own campaign.

## 5. BEFORE YOU LAUNCH — ACTION ITEMS FOR HUGO

**Do these in order. Do not run ads until item 1 is resolved.**

1. **CRITICAL — Verify reflexsystem.com domain status.** During research, this domain appeared parked on GoDaddy. If someone clicks your LinkedIn ad and lands on a parked page, every dollar spent is wasted. Confirm the website is live, loads correctly, and has a working lead capture form or landing page before any campaign goes live.

2. **Build the landing page for Offer A (Free Benchmark Report).** The LinkedIn Lead Gen Form is pre-built (in the copy deck), but you also need a landing page version for tracking and retargeting. Minimum: headline, 3 bullet value points, form. Can be built in one hour on any page builder.

3. **Produce the Benchmark Report asset.** This is the PDF or document that leads receive after filling out Offer A. It does not need to be long — 5-7 pages, branded, with data on closing rate benchmarks for US dealerships is enough. This is what gets opened, which is where trust is built.

4. **Set up LinkedIn Campaign Manager.** Confirm the Reflex business page exists on LinkedIn, has a profile photo and banner, and has at least 5-10 followers before running ads. LinkedIn ads perform measurably worse from pages with no activity.

5. **Assign a human to respond to Offer B leads within 24 hours.** The Objection Audit is a 20-minute call. Leads who book this have already decided they are interested. A slow response kills the deal. Define who takes the call and how it is booked (Calendly link or similar).

6. **Install LinkedIn Insight Tag on reflexsystem.com.** This is a small tracking code LinkedIn provides. It takes 10 minutes to install. Without it, you cannot track which leads became deals, and you cannot build retargeting audiences for Phase 2.

7. **Pull actual closing rate data from current Reflex clients (if available).** The strongest possible ad you can run says: "Our clients averaged a 22% improvement in closing rate in 30 days." Any real number from a real client beats all the copy in the deck. If you have even one data point, use it.

## 6. BUDGET RECOMMENDATION

**Suggested starting budget: $4,000/month for Phase 1**

This covers:
- Campaign 1 (GM/DP — Document Ads): $2,000/month (50%)
- Campaign 2 (DP — ROI Math): $1,200/month (30%)
- Campaign 3 (Sales Manager — Single Image): $800/month (20%)

**Why $4,000 and not less:**
LinkedIn's minimum to exit the learning period and get meaningful data is roughly $1,500 per campaign per month. Below that, the algorithm never stabilizes and you get noise, not data. At $4,000 total across 3 campaigns, each campaign has enough spend to learn.

**Why $4,000 and not more:**
You have zero performance data yet. Spend at this level for 30-45 days, identify which campaign and which ad is winning, then increase budget on the winner only.

**When to increase:**
- Do not touch budgets for the first 7 days (learning period)
- At Day 8, if any campaign has a CPL below $250, increase that campaign by 20%
- At Day 30, if CPL is consistently below $200, increase monthly budget to $6,000 and shift allocation toward the winning campaign
- At Day 60, evaluate the ABM play (Phase 2) — budget separately at $1,500-2,000/month for enterprise group targeting

**Kill condition:** If you reach $500 spent on any single campaign with zero leads, pause that campaign before spending more. Diagnose before continuing.
