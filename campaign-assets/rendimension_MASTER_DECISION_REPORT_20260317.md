## THE 30-SECOND VERDICT

Don't advertise "3D rendering." That market is dead — flooded by $200 outsource shops and AI tools that dominate Facebook ads. Instead, advertise what renders DO for a business: close deals, get funding, accelerate approvals, prevent costly mistakes. The data confirms 3 blue ocean territories with almost ZERO advertising competition and $15K–$100K+ ticket sizes.

## DECISION 4: What Ad Formats to Use

### What Competitors Run (and why to do differently)

| What They Do | Why It Fails |
|---|---|
| 90%+ single image (hero render) | Looks like portfolio, not advertising. No story. No business context. |
| Occasional carousel (project showcase) | Still portfolio-based. No pain point. No outcome. |
| Very rare video | Walkthroughs without context. No human element. |
| Zero document ads | Nobody is educating the market |

### What Rendimension Should Run

| Format | Purpose | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Video (short-form, 15-30s)** | Show the DECISION moment, not the render | Archsynth proves video + storytelling = longest-running ads |
| **Carousel** | "5 mistakes developers make with cheap renders" (education) | Nobody does educational content. Carousel = engagement format. |
| **Document Ad** | "The ROI of Architectural Visualization — Real Numbers" | Zero competition in document ads for archviz. Authority builder. |
| **Single Image** | The human reaction shot — boardroom, approval, handshake | NOT the building. The MOMENT the visualization changes a decision. |

### From the Facebook Ad Library (what's running and surviving)

| Advertiser | Strategy | Duration | Why It's Working |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Archsynth** (350+ ads, 10 months) | Pain-point storytelling, text message screenshots, emotional hooks | Since Jun 2025 | Storytelling > portfolio. They don't show renders — they show frustration. |

| **CloudPano** (dominates 360 tours) | SaaS positioning, free trial, tool-focused | Long-running | Proves the "tool" market is taken — studios must sell outcomes. |

### What This Means for Rendimension

1. **Storytelling beats portfolio.** Archsynth's 10-month ad run proves emotional, pain-point copy outperforms feature lists.
2. **Business outcomes beat beautiful images.** The Render Pros is the ONLY studio using "$30M sellouts" language. Nobody else does this.
3. **Premium studios don't advertise on Facebook.** Neoscape, DBOX, Steelblue, Brick Visual — NONE are in the Ad Library. This means the Facebook ad space for premium visualization is COMPLETELY OPEN.
4. **AI tools own the "rendering" keyword.** Archsynth, mnml.ai, ArchFine dominate. Don't compete with them on "rendering" — compete on what AI CAN'T do: strategic visual storytelling for high-stakes decisions.

## FINAL STRATEGIC RECOMMENDATION

### The Play: "Visualization That Closes Deals"

Rendimension should NOT advertise as a render studio. It should advertise as the partner that helps developers, investors, and luxury concept owners make confident multi-million dollar decisions through visual storytelling.

### Campaign Architecture (3 campaigns, phased)

**Campaign 1: "Pre-Sell Before You Build" (Launch first)**
- Target: Real estate developers, $5M+ projects
- Message: Your project can generate revenue before construction starts
- Proof: Industry data (60% pre-sales, 47 fewer days in approvals, 25% faster funding)
- Format: Video + Carousel + Document Ad
- Budget allocation: 50%

**Campaign 2: "See the Investment Before You Make It" (Launch second)**
- Target: Commercial investors, luxury concept developers
- Message: Before you invest millions, walk through the experience
- Proof: Hugo's Miami case + experiential concept examples
- Format: Video + Single Image (human reaction shots)
- Budget allocation: 30%

**Campaign 3: "Stop Paying Twice" — Anti-Outsource (Launch third)**
- Target: Developers who've been burned by cheap rendering
- Message: The $200 render cost you $20,000. Here's what happens when you invest right.
- Proof: Before/after quality + business outcome comparison
- Format: Carousel (comparison) + Document Ad (guide)
- Budget allocation: 20%
