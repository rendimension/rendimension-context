## VIDEO 1: "The Pre-Sale Advantage" (30 seconds)
**Hook:** Pre-Sold Before Groundbreaking (Hook 3)
**Format:** Avatar speaking to camera + B-roll overlays
**Use:** Test AFTER static image ads prove which hook wins

### Script

| Time | What's on Screen | Avatar Script (spoken) | Text Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-3s | Avatar looking at camera, slight pause | "What if I told you that 60% of your units could be sold... before you break ground?" | 60% PRE-SOLD |
| 3-8s | Cut to: photorealistic render of luxury development (use Nano Banana image) | "Smart developers aren't waiting for construction to start selling. They're using photorealistic 3D visualization to let buyers walk through the project before it exists." | — |
| 8-14s | Cut to: split screen - empty lot on left, finished render on right | "Your buyers can't feel a blueprint. They can't picture a floor plan. But when they see THIS — they commit." | — |
| 14-20s | Avatar back on camera | "We've built visualization packages for over 1,000 projects. Renders. Walkthroughs. Investor presentations. Everything a buyer needs to say yes." | 1,000+ Projects |
| 20-26s | Cut to: render of sales center with RESERVED tags on unit board | "Your competitor already has deposits in the bank. The question is — do you?" | — |
| 26-30s | Avatar on camera, confident close | "Get a free project estimate. Link below." | GET YOUR ESTIMATE (arrow down) |

### HeyGen Settings
- **Avatar:** Professional male, 30s-40s, business casual (sport coat, no tie)
- **Background:** Modern office or neutral gradient
- **Tone:** Confident, direct, no fluff — like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson
- **Pace:** Slightly faster than conversational — urgency without rushing
- **Music:** Subtle corporate background, low volume

### B-Roll Needed (Generate with Nano Banana)
1. Photorealistic luxury development render (exterior, evening lighting, people walking)
2. Split screen: empty lot vs finished render (same angle)
3. Modern sales center interior with unit availability board showing RESERVED tags

### HeyGen Settings
- **Avatar:** Same as Video 1 (brand consistency)

## VIDEO 3: "Why Your Project Isn't Moving" (20 seconds - Short Format)
**Hook:** The Empty Lot Problem (Hook 1)
**Format:** No avatar — text + visuals only (Reels/Stories format)
**Use:** For Instagram Reels placement or Stories

### Script

| Time | What's on Screen | Audio | Text Overlay |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0-2s | Empty construction lot, gray sky, fence | Dramatic music hit | Your project is stuck. |
| 2-5s | Zoom into blueprints on a table, flat, boring | Music builds | It's not the financing. It's not the location. |
| 5-8s | Transition wipe: blueprints transform into photorealistic 3D render | Music shift to positive | It's that nobody can PICTURE what you're building. |
| 8-12s | Slow pan across stunning interior render (living room, luxury finishes) | Music continues positive | When they can SEE it — they commit. |
| 12-16s | Quick montage: 3 different render angles (exterior, interior, aerial) | Music peak | 1,000+ projects delivered |
| 16-20s | Rendimension logo + CTA | Music fade | GET YOUR FREE ESTIMATE → Link in bio |

### Production Notes
- This video is ALL visuals + text overlays — no avatar
- Can be created in HeyGen using their text-to-video or slideshow feature
- Or assembled manually with Nano Banana images + CapCut/Canva video editor
- Vertical format: 1080x1920 (9:16) for Reels/Stories
- Also create 1080x1080 (1:1) version for Feed

### Nano Banana Images Needed
1. Empty construction lot (gray, desolate, fence, overcast)
2. Blueprints on a table (flat, technical, unexciting)
3. Luxury interior render (living room, warm lighting, detailed finishes)
4. Exterior render (evening, people, landscaping)
5. Aerial/bird's eye render of a development
