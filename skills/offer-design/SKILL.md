# Offer Design

## The Offer Equation
**Irresistible Offer = Desired Result + Clear Timeframe + Risk Reversal**

Bad: "We offer 3D rendering services"
Good: "See your project in photorealistic 3D before construction — ready in 5 days, free initial consultation"

## Offer Hierarchy

### Level 1: Lead Magnet (Free)
- Rendimension: Free consultation, free sample render, free visualization assessment
- Reflex: Free dealership performance audit, free VR demo, free training gap assessment

### Level 2: Tripwire (Low Cost)
- Rendimension: Single room render at $499, limited VR walkthrough
- Reflex: Single rep assessment, pilot program for 5 reps

### Level 3: Core Offer (Main Product)
- Rendimension: Full project visualization package
- Reflex: Full dealership VR training subscription

### Level 4: Premium (High Ticket)
- Rendimension: Multi-property visualization, ongoing virtual staging
- Reflex: Enterprise multi-dealership licensing, custom scenarios

## Testing Rules
1. Test 2-3 offers simultaneously
2. Same audience, same creative, different offer
3. Run minimum 7 days
4. Winner = lowest CPL (not highest CTR)
5. Document in winner-pattern-library
