# Pain Point Mapping

## Severity x Frequency Matrix

|  | Low Frequency | Medium Frequency | High Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **High Severity** | Awareness ads | Strong ad angles | BEST ad angles |
| **Medium Severity** | Skip | Decent angles | Good angles |
| **Low Severity** | Skip | Skip | Content only |

## Rendimension Pain Points

| Pain Point | Who | When | Current Solution | Cost | Ad Angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clients can't visualize finished space | Architects | Client presentations | 2D renders, mood boards | Lost projects ($50K-$500K) | "Walk through the design before you build it" |
| Pre-sale units don't sell fast enough | Developers | Pre-construction marketing | Floor plans, model units ($200K+) | Delayed revenue | "Pre-sell 3X faster with 3D tours" |
| Costly change orders from misunderstanding | Builders | Mid-construction | RFIs, meetings, rework | $10K-$100K per change | "Catch problems before they cost $100K" |
| Competitive pitches lost to better presentations | Architects | Competitive bids | PowerPoint, basic renders | Lost revenue | "Win more pitches with cinematic 3D" |
| Luxury buyers need to see quality | Developers | High-end sales | Model homes ($200K-$500K each) | High upfront cost | "Show luxury in 3D — no model home needed" |

## Reflex Pain Points

| Pain Point | Who | When | Current Solution | Cost | Ad Angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low closing rate | Dealer Principals | Monthly P&L review | Hire more reps | Lost revenue $500K+/year | "What's one extra sale per rep per month worth?" |
| Training doesn't stick | Sales Managers | After training events | Classroom, ride-alongs | Training budget wasted | "Reps forget 90% in 30 days — unless they practice" |
| Can't measure rep skills objectively | Sales Managers | Performance reviews | Gut feeling, ride-alongs | Promoting wrong people | "See where each rep struggles — with data" |
| Inconsistent performance across dealerships | Group VPs | Quarterly reviews | Different trainers | Revenue variance $1M+ | "Standardize training. Standardize results." |
| High turnover in sales team | Dealer Principals | Ongoing | Constant recruiting | $10K-$30K per turnover | "Reps who feel prepared stay longer" |

## Pain-to-Copy Formula
**Pain Point → Agitate → Solution → CTA**

Example (Rendimension):
- Pain: "Clients can't visualize the finished space"
- Agitate: "Competitors show 3D walkthroughs while you show floor plans"
- Solution: "Cinematic 3D visualizations that let clients experience the space"
- CTA: "Book a free visualization consultation"
