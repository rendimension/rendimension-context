# Direct Response B2B

## Core: Get the reader to take the NEXT step. Not buy. Not commit. Just the next step.

## PAS Framework
1. **Problem**: State one they recognize immediately
2. **Agitate**: Make the cost of NOT solving it visceral
3. **Solve**: Present your solution as the obvious answer

## AIDA Framework
1. **Attention**: Hook with something unexpected
2. **Interest**: Connect to their situation
3. **Desire**: Paint life with your solution
4. **Action**: Exact next step

## Objection Pre-emption

### Rendimension
- "Too expensive" → "Save $50K+ in avoided change orders"
- "Takes too long" → "Delivered in 5 business days"
- "Won't look real" → "This isn't a photo. It's our render."

### Reflex
- "Team won't use it" → "92% rep engagement rate"
- "Training doesn't work" → "This isn't training. It's practice."
- "Hard to measure ROI" → "Every session scored. Every improvement tracked."

## Strong CTAs
- "Book Your Free Consultation" (specific + free)
- "See Your Project in 3D" (outcome-focused)
- "Request a Live Demo" (low commitment)
- "Calculate Your ROI" (value-focused)

## Weak CTAs (avoid)
- "Contact Us", "Learn More", "Submit", "Click Here"
