# LinkedIn Ad Copywriter

## Character Limits
- Intro Text: 150 chars visible (max 600)
- Headline: 70 chars recommended (max 200)
- Description: 100 chars (max 300)

## Professional Tone Rules
1. Confident, not salesy
2. Data-driven, not emotional
3. Speak to the role: "Sales Managers" not "Hey there"
4. Industry language when appropriate
5. Lead with business impact
6. No emojis in headlines
