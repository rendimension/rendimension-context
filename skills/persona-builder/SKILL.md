## Rendimension Personas

### "Developer Dave"
- **Role**: Real Estate Developer / VP of Development
- **Company**: Mid-size dev firm, 20-200 employees, $10M-$200M
- **Age**: 38-55 | **Location**: TX, FL, CA
- **Goals**: Pre-sell faster, reduce risk, differentiate projects
- **Frustrations**: Floor plans don't excite buyers, model units expensive, competitors have better marketing
- **Objections**: "How much?", "How long?", "Will it look real?"
- **Triggers**: New project starting, slow pre-sales, investor presentation
- **Format**: Video walkthrough, carousel | **Angle**: "Pre-sell 3X faster with 3D"

### "Architect Anna"
- **Role**: Principal Architect / Design Director
- **Company**: Architecture firm, 5-50 employees
- **Age**: 32-50 | **Location**: Major US metros
- **Goals**: Win pitches, wow clients, differentiate firm
- **Frustrations**: Clients don't understand 2D, losing to better visuals
- **Objections**: "We render in-house", "Too expensive", "Timeline too tight"
- **Triggers**: Competitive pitch, major presentation, portfolio refresh
- **Format**: Hero image, before/after carousel | **Angle**: "Win every pitch with cinematic 3D"

## Reflex Personas

### "Principal Pete"
- **Role**: Dealer Principal / Owner
- **Company**: Single or multi-dealership, 50-500 employees
- **Age**: 45-65 | **Location**: USA
- **Goals**: Increase closing rate, reduce turnover, grow revenue
- **Frustrations**: Training doesn't stick, can't find good reps, inconsistent performance
- **Objections**: "My team won't use it", "We tried VR", "How to measure ROI?"
- **Triggers**: Bad P&L month, lost key salesperson, competitor growing
- **Format**: Case study, ROI image | **Angle**: "One extra sale per rep per month"

### "Manager Maria"
- **Role**: Sales Manager
- **Company**: Dealership, 50-200 employees
- **Age**: 30-50 | **Location**: USA
- **Goals**: Hit monthly targets, develop reps, reduce coaching time
- **Frustrations**: Reps resist roleplay, can't measure skills, training vs selling time
- **Objections**: "Reps too busy", "Won't take it seriously", "Different from video training?"
- **Triggers**: Missed target, new hire onboarding, GM pressure
- **Format**: Demo video, document carousel | **Angle**: "VR roleplay they'll actually do"
