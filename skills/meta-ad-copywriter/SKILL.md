# Meta Ad Copywriter

## Character Limits
- Primary Text: 125 chars visible before "See More"
- Headline: 40 chars recommended
- Description: 30 chars recommended

## Hook Formulas
1. "What if you could [outcome] before [risk]?"
2. "[Number]% of [audience] [shocking truth]."
3. "Most [audience] think [belief]. They're wrong."
4. "[Time ago], [persona] was [problem]. Then [transformation]."
5. "[Result] in [timeframe]. Here's how."
6. "This isn't a photo. It's a 3D render."
7. "Your competitors already [do this]. Do you?"
8. "If you're tired of [pain], you're not alone."
9. "[Client type] [achieved result] using [solution]."
10. "[Thing A] costs $X. [Our thing] costs $Y. Same result."

## Primary Text Structures
### PAS: Problem → Agitate → Solve → CTA
### Story: Mini-story → Complication → Resolution → CTA
### Direct: Offer → Benefit → Proof → CTA

## A/B Testing
- Test ONE variable at a time
- 1,000 impressions minimum per variant
- Winner = lower CPL, not higher CTR
