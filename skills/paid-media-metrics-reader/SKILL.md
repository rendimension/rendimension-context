# Paid Media Metrics Reader

## Metrics in Plain Language

| Metric | What It Means | Meta Good | Meta Bad | LinkedIn Good | LinkedIn Bad |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CTR | % who clicked | >2% | <0.8% | >0.5% | <0.3% |
| CPC | Cost per click | <$1.50 | >$3.00 | <$8 | >$15 |
| CPL | Cost per lead | <$80 | >$150 | <$250 | >$400 |
| CPM | Cost per 1K views | <$30 | >$60 | <$40 | >$80 |
| Conv Rate | % clicks → leads | >5% | <2% | >3% | <1% |
| Frequency | Times same person saw ad | <3 good | >5 bad | <3 good | >4 bad |

## Diagnostic Trees

**Low CTR**: Hook compelling? → Visual stopping scroll? → Audience right? → Frequency high?
**High CPC**: CTR low? Fix ad. Competition high? Try different audience. Wrong placements? Adjust.
**High CPL**: CPC high? Fix ad. Landing page converting? Fix page. Offer compelling? Test different.
**Low Conversion**: Message match? Page speed? Form length? Offer clarity?
