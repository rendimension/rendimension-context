# Weekly Campaign Report Template

```
WEEKLY AD CAMPAIGN REPORT — Week of [date]

THE BIG PICTURE
Total Spent: $X | Leads: X | Avg CPL: $X
Verdict: [One sentence]

RENDIMENSION (Meta)
Spent: $X | Leads: X | CPL: $X [GREEN/YELLOW/RED]
Best: "[headline]" — [format] | CTR X% | CPL $X — Why: [1 sentence]
Worst: "[headline]" — [format] | CTR X% | CPL $X — Problem: [1 sentence]
vs Last Week: Leads [up/down %], CPL [up/down %]
Actions: 1. [specific] 2. [specific]

REFLEX (LinkedIn)
[Same structure]

DECISIONS NEEDED: [with recommendations]
NEXT WEEK: [planned actions]
```

## Example Report
Rendimension: Spent $350, 8 leads, $43.75/lead GREEN. Best: "See Your Kitchen" carousel, 3.1% CTR. Worst: "3D Rendering Services" 0.7% CTR. Action: Pause worst, scale best 20%.
Reflex: Spent $470, 6 leads, $78.33/lead GREEN. Best: "Losing Deals" single image, 0.62% CTR. Action: Create document carousel test.

## Rules: 2-min read max, traffic lights, specific actions, no jargon.
