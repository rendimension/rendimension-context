# Simple Executive Summary

## 5-Sentence Format
1. How much did we spend?
2. What did we get?
3. What worked best?
4. What's the biggest problem?
5. What should we do?

## Example
"We spent $1,400 on Meta ads in March. That generated 32 leads at $43.75 each — well below our $80 target. The luxury kitchen carousel had a 3.1% click rate. Architect-focused ads aren't converting — the offer might not resonate. Next step: test a free design review offer for architects."

## ROI Calculation
ROI = (Revenue from leads - Ad spend) / Ad spend × 100
Example: $40K revenue from $4.2K spend = 852% ROI = "$9.52 back for every $1 spent"
