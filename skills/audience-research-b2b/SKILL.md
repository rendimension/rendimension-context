# B2B Audience Research

## Research Framework

### Step 1: Identify the Buying Committee
- **Decision Maker**: Has budget authority (Owner, VP, C-Suite)
- **Influencer**: Recommends solutions (Manager, Director)
- **User**: Will use the product daily (Staff, Team Lead)
- **Gatekeeper**: Controls access (Admin, Procurement)

### Step 2: Map Pain Points
For each persona, document:

| Pain Point | Severity (1-5) | Frequency | Current Solution | Ad Angle |
|---|---|---|---|---|

### Step 3: Buying Cycle Mapping

| Stage | What They Do | What They Need | Ad Approach |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Realize they have a problem | Education, perspective | Thought leadership, pain point ads |
| Consideration | Research solutions | Comparisons, case studies | Demo ads, carousel explainers |
| Decision | Choose a vendor | Proof, risk reduction | Testimonials, offers, trials |

### Step 4: Communication Channels
- Where do they spend time online?
- What content do they consume?
- What language/jargon do they use?
- What publications/groups do they follow?

### Step 5: Identify Objections
Common objections and how to address them in ads.

## Pre-Built Audience Profiles

### Rendimension Audiences

**Segment A: Real Estate Developers**
- Company: 10-500 employees, $5M-$500M revenue
- Trigger: new project starting, pre-sales needed
- Pain: Can't visualize finished product for buyers
- Angle: "Pre-sell units before construction"

**Segment B: Architects**
- Company: 5-100 employees
- Trigger: competitive pitch, client presentation
- Pain: Clients can't understand 2D plans
- Angle: "Win more projects with cinematic presentations"

**Segment C: Construction Companies**
- Company: 20-500 employees
- Trigger: change orders, design miscommunication
- Pain: Expensive rework from misunderstanding
- Angle: "Eliminate costly change orders"

### Reflex Audiences

**Segment A: Dealer Principals/Owners**
- Company: 50-5000 employees
- Trigger: low closing rates, high turnover
- Pain: Inconsistent sales performance
- Angle: "What if every rep performed like your top closer?"

**Segment B: Sales Managers**
- Company: 50-500 employees
- Trigger: coaching doesn't stick, can't measure
- Pain: Training takes time from selling
- Angle: "Practice handling real objections in VR"

**Segment C: Automotive Group VPs**
- Company: 500-10000 employees
- Trigger: standardize across dealerships
- Pain: Can't scale quality training
- Angle: "Standardize sales excellence across every dealership"
