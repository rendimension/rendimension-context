# LinkedIn Campaign Builder

## Structure: Campaign Group → Campaign → Ad

## Objectives: Lead Generation (recommended with Lead Gen Forms) | Website Visits | Engagement

## Budget: $50-80/day testing → $80-150 optimizing → $150-300 scaling
## Expected CPL: $150-300 testing → $100-200 optimizing → $80-150 scaling

## Reflex Targeting
**Job Titles (Tier 1)**: Dealer Principal, Dealership Owner, General Manager
**Job Titles (Tier 2)**: Sales Manager, Director of Sales, VP of Sales, Director of Training
**Industry**: Automotive, Retail Motor Vehicles
**Company Size**: 51-200, 201-500, 501-1000, 1001-5000
**Seniority**: Manager, Director, VP, CXO, Owner
**Geography**: United States
**Audience size**: 50K-300K ideal

## Lead Gen Form Fields
First Name, Last Name, Email, Job Title, Company Name (all pre-filled) + "How many salespeople?" (dropdown)

## Launch Checklist
- [ ] Insight Tag installed on reflexsystem.com
- [ ] Conversion tracking configured
- [ ] Audience 50K-300K
- [ ] Lead Gen Form or landing page ready
- [ ] Budget approved
- [ ] Creative uploaded + previewed
- [ ] UTMs configured
- [ ] Desktop + mobile preview checked
