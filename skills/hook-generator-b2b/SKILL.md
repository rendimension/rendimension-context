# B2B Hook Generator

## Question Hooks
1. "What if you could [outcome] without [sacrifice]?"
   - Rendimension: "What if you could see your project before investing in construction?"
   - Reflex: "What if you could predict which reps will miss quota?"

2. "How much is [problem] costing your [business]?"
   - Rendimension: "How much are change orders costing your projects?"
   - Reflex: "How much revenue is poor objection handling costing you?"

## Stat Hooks
3. "[%] of [audience] [negative fact]."
   - Rendimension: "73% of projects have change orders exceeding $50K."
   - Reflex: "68% of dealership sales are lost to poor objection handling."

4. "[Audience] who [action] see [result]."
   - Rendimension: "Developers using 3D visualization pre-sell 3X faster."
   - Reflex: "Dealerships using VR roleplay see 23% higher closing rates."

## Contrarian Hooks
5. "Stop [common advice]. Here's what actually works."
   - Rendimension: "Stop showing floor plans to luxury buyers."
   - Reflex: "Stop sending your team to sales seminars."

## Story Hooks
6. "Last [time], [persona] was [problem]. Then [transformation]."
   - Rendimension: "Last quarter, a developer struggled to pre-sell. Then 3D walkthroughs. 80% sold."
   - Reflex: "Last month, 3 reps under quota. After VR roleplay, all 3 hit target."

## Pain Hooks
7. "Tired of [pain]? You're not alone."
8. "If [pain situation], it's not your fault. But it IS your problem."

## Aspiration Hooks
9. "Imagine [aspirational scenario]."
10. "The best [audience] in the world all [do this]."

## Fear of Missing Hooks
11. "Your competitors are already [doing this]. Are you?"
12. "In 2 years, every [audience] will [use this]. Early adopters win."

## Result Hooks
13. "[Specific result] in [timeframe]. No [objection]."
14. "[Client] went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]."

## Curiosity Hooks
15. "The #1 reason [audience] [fail at thing] has nothing to do with [obvious cause]."
16. "We showed this to [audience]. Their reaction was [unexpected]."

## Testing: Write 5-10 hooks, test 3 at a time, 1,000 impressions min, winner = highest CTR.
