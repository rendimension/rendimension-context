# LinkedIn Creative Matrix

## Algorithm Preferences
1. Native content preferred over external links
2. Document/carousel posts get high organic reach
3. Video gets priority in feed
4. Professional tone expected but personality welcomed

## B2B Formats That Convert

### Single Image — Clean, one message, bold headline. For: awareness, retargeting, lead gen
### Document Carousel — 5-7 slides PDF. Progressive narrative. For: education, authority
### Video — 30-90s, captions required. For: consideration, lead gen
### Conversation Ads — Personal InMail. For: warm audiences only

## Document Carousel Structure (Reflex Example)
1. Cover: "5 Reasons Your Sales Training Isn't Working"
2. Problem 1: Passive learning + stat
3. Problem 2: No practice + consequence
4. Problem 3: Can't measure + what it means
5. Solution: "VR roleplay solves all 5"
6. CTA: Book demo + URL

## Reflex Language Rules
- Say "closing rate" not "conversion rate"
- Say "reps" not "users"
- Say "dealership" not "company"
- Use: F&I, desk deals, trade walk, objection handling
- ROI: "per rep per month" "annual revenue impact"

## Targeting Best Practices
1. Start broader (50K-300K audience)
2. Let algorithm optimize before narrowing
3. Layer: job title + industry (not too many filters)
4. Exclude current customers and competitors
5. Test job title vs job function targeting
