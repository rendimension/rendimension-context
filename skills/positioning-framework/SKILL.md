# Positioning Framework

## Template
"For [TARGET] who [NEED], [BRAND] is the [CATEGORY] that [BENEFIT], unlike [ALTERNATIVES], because [REASON TO BELIEVE]."

**For Builders:** "For construction companies losing money on miscommunication, Rendimension is the visualization service that catches problems before they become change orders, unlike 2D plans, because our renders leave no room for misinterpretation."
